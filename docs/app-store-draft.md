## Promotional text

Make cooking for yourself a little easier. Keep favorite recipes, choose meals for your week, and take a grocery list with you.

## Description

Your week, made easy.

Table for One helps you decide what to cook and what to buy when you're cooking for yourself.

- Browse recipes and keep your favorites in your own cookbook.
- Pick breakfast, lunch, dinner and side options for this week or a future week. Cook them whenever you like.
- Read ingredients and preparation steps, or follow along in cooking mode.
- Keep a simple pantry list and reuse your regular grocery purchases.
- Add your weekly recipe ingredients to a grocery list, choose purchase quantities, and add your own items.
- Group groceries by section, leave notes and check off purchases. Keep editing your cached grocery list offline and sync when you reconnect.
- Set a weekly grocery budget and record what you actually spent.
- Sign in on iPhone and iPad, then refresh to load your latest account data.

Cooking measurements stay with the recipe. When a shopping quantity is unknown, you choose what to buy. Changing your weekly meal picks leaves existing groceries available for you to review and edit.

## Reviewer notes draft

The main destinations are Week, Discover, Saved, Groceries and Settings. Profile, pantry and account deletion are in Settings. Find a recipe opens Discover; adding a recipe to a week preserves it in Saved when later removed from that week.

To exercise offline shopping, sign in, open Groceries online to cache the selected week, disconnect, edit/check items, and reconnect to synchronize. Account-backed actions require a working service and reviewer account. Supply and verify reviewer access separately before submission.
